# Plates

- `fold-d.webp`, `fold-l.webp`: **Fold**, the GNOME 51 default wallpaper (dark and light), by the
  GNOME Design Team, from gnome-backgrounds 51.0.1, licensed
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Decoded from
  `/usr/share/backgrounds/gnome/fold-{d,l}.jxl` and cropped from 4096x4096 to 1920x1080 at the
  centre, the way GNOME's "zoom" option fills a 16:9 screen. The title scene's frames and videos
  are adaptations of it and are shared under CC BY-SA 3.0 too.
- `desktop-dark.webp`, `desktop-light.webp`: stand-ins for the montage's first and last frames,
  the showcase screenshots of this desktop (`docs/showcase/media/desktop.webp`,
  `light-desktop.webp` on the showcase branch). They are replaced by frames of the VM recordings.
